# BCG Campaign Performance Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the BCG Forage Introduction to Data for Decision Makers job simulation.

The project involved analyzing the performance of a recent digital advertising campaign for NewCo, a large big-box retailer. The goal was to evaluate campaign and channel performance, identify what drove new-customer engagement, and provide data-driven recommendations for future marketing investment.

##  Business Objectives

The analysis focused on answering three key questions:

- Which campaign and channel combination delivered the most value?
- What drove engagement and sales from new customers?
- Where should marketing investment be focused in the next quarter?

## Analysis Performed

I analyzed campaign performance across different marketing channels and compared:

- Campaign performance
- Channel performance
- Total sales
- New-customer sales
- Campaign and channel combinations
- Sales-focused and brand-focused messaging

I used Excel and pivot tables to organize, summarize, and analyze the campaign data.

## Key Findings

- Campaign A + Email generated the highest new-customer sales at $2,124.47.
- Campaign A + Instagram generated $969.46 in new-customer sales.
- Campaign A + Web Banner generated $633.06 in new-customer sales.
- Campaign A generated $5,869.56 in total sales.
- Campaign B generated $8,790.04 in total sales.

Although Campaign B generated higher overall sales, Campaign A performed better in attracting new customers.

## Key Insight

The analysis showed that campaign success should be evaluated based on the specific business objective rather than total sales alone.

While Campaign B generated higher overall sales, Campaign A demonstrated stronger potential for new-customer acquisition, with Email being its strongest-performing channel for new-customer sales.

## Recommendation

For future marketing investment focused on new-customer acquisition, the business should consider prioritizing the stronger-performing Campaign A channels, particularly Email.

Marketing performance should also continue to be evaluated by campaign, channel, and customer type to ensure investment decisions are aligned with the desired business outcome.
